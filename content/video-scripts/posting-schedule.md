# 4-Week Posting Schedule — USIG Video Content Library

30 scripts (15 USIG.ai + 15 biz.usig.ai) spread across 4 weeks at roughly
2 posts/day on weekdays. Mix categories daily so no single platform feed
gets saturated with one topic — alternate USIG.ai (residential/commercial/
financing) and biz.usig.ai (business advisory) day to day.

Cadence: **Mon–Fri, 2 videos/day** (10/week) → 30 videos ≈ **3 weeks** of
daily posting, with week 4 held as a buffer for re-cuts, top-performer ad
promotion, and any scripts still in production.

## Platform mix per post

Post the same finished video everywhere same-day, cropped per platform:

| Platform | Format |
|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | 9:16 |
| TikTok | 9:16 |
| YouTube Shorts | 9:16 |
| Facebook | 1:1 (feed) or 9:16 (Stories/Reels) |
| LinkedIn | 1:1 |
